About

I've been building games on Roblox since fifth grade, now over twelve years ago, starting with shooters and gradually taking on more complex systems across team and solo projects. Over that time I became deeply embedded in the Roblox development community, regularly helping others debug and learn on the forums and through the RTC, which sharpened both my technical instincts and my ability to communicate clearly about code. My most significant work to date is a contract role on Heroes of Robloxia 2, where I architected a versatile enemy AI system built on a state-machine/behavior-tree hybrid, along with weapon systems and a custom 3D particle system. I hold a Bachelor's degree in Software Engineering with a minor in Mathematics from MSOE, and while Lua is the language I know most deeply, the software engineering principles I studied in college (architecture, clean code, and rigorous problem-solving) show clearly in how I approach my Roblox work. I'm someone who has always learned by doing, and I'm as comfortable diving into creating a new system from scratch as I am jumping into an existing codebase.
By the way, props to you for reading the history of this repo! I hope to work with you soon!
